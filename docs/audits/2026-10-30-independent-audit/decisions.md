# Fermata audit: decisions only Nuno can make

24 decisions, each tied to ledger rows (`ledger.md`). Every recommendation says whose it is; where the sources give none, that is stated. Decision D-01 costs nothing and unblocks most of the guitar work, so it comes first.

## D-01 · Ask Zoltán before the guitar work starts

**Question.** Ask your teacher for his scale fingerings, the position names he uses, and whether he uses solfège or Hungarian note naming.

**Options.**
- Ask now (zero cost)
- Skip and let the app choose

**Recommendation.** Ask now. Track G names this the binding constraint for the position mode, fingered scale routes and teacher override; building those first risks redoing them.

**Ledger rows.** W3-01, W6-06, W10-03, W10-05

## D-02 · Guitar position system and default fretboard view

**Question.** What should the 'Scale Pos' chips be, and what should the fretboard show by default?

**Options.**
- (a) Classical positions generated by the app (Roman numerals, 4-fret window, open position, finger numbers) as the default; rock/CAGED boxes behind a toggle
- (b) Same as (a) but remove CAGED boxes
- (c) Keep CAGED naming but regenerate the boxes correctly
- (d) Keep the whole neck as the default, positions opt-in
- Also: keep the chosen position when the key changes (yes/no)

**Recommendation.** Whatever the naming, stop using the hand-made data: the auditors (A-1) and Track G agree positions should be generated from the scale formula, and G advises against fixing the CAGED data in place. Track G recommends (a), with the whole neck kept as a reference view rather than the practice default. Keeping the position across key changes is cheap (A-30, F-14).

**Ledger rows.** W3-01, W3-02, W3-06, W3-08, W3-13, W10-04, W10-07
