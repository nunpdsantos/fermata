Fermata Audit

Seven independent tracks examined everything Fermata draws, plays, teaches and grades, plus a live walkthrough. A second model family re-checked every serious finding. This page holds the result: what is wrong, what to fix first, and the decisions only you can make.

The live site runs exactly this code (files byte-identical to a fresh build). Nothing in the app has been changed.

Summary

Avoid this until it is fixed: on the live site, do not choose the scales Dim W-H, Dim H-W, Bebop Dom or Bebop Maj. The whole app crashes, stays crashed after reload, and the only way out it offers deletes your saved progress.

Three things stand out. First, what the instruments and staff show had never been checked against the music theory, and that is where the worst errors are: 74 of 85 guitar scale positions are wrong, the circle of fifths gives the wrong key for most scales, and the staff draws some notes wrongly. Second, some Learn exercises cannot be answered or mark right answers wrong, and generated questions in about 25 modules test another module's topic. Third, English corrections made between March and July never reached Portuguese and Spanish, so those versions still teach the old mistakes.

The music engine underneath is sound. All 1,315 Drill items, about 4,900 chord-symbol inputs and all 870 Learn answer keys were checked in full against independent calculations, and outside the listed items they are right. Type checks, 5,670 unit tests, lint and the build all pass.

      Compared with the July audit

      Method and limits

      Who did what. Seven auditor tracks ran on Claude Opus 5.5: instrument displays, Learn levels 1 to 5, Learn levels 6 to 9 with Explore, Drill and grading, Portuguese and Spanish, engineering, and classical-guitar fit. I did the live walkthrough and wrote this report, also on Opus 5.5. Eight checkers on Claude Sonnet 5.5, a different model family, re-tried every serious and medium finding from source: 151 checked, 107 confirmed, 44 real but with smaller scope or severity, none rejected. Fable was not used. The subagents used about 4.9 million tokens in total.

      Evidence. Every finding cites the file and line, plus a script whose output shows it or a step-by-step music-theory derivation. Each checking script was first shown to catch a deliberately planted error. The 85 minor issues marked “not re-checked” were not re-tried by a second model.

      Not covered. Phone-size layout was measured in an emulator, not on a phone, and iOS Safari and the installed app were not tested. Sound quality was not judged, because an agent cannot listen. The Portuguese and Spanish review is not a native speaker's review. Exam-syllabus details come from the boards' published documents and carry their own currency caveats.

      One incident. To confirm the crash, the instrument auditor opened the live site in your browser, crashed your saved state there, and then restored it. Afterwards the live app loaded normally, and your progress and Drill data were present at exactly the sizes recorded before the test. Whether the restored key and scale match what you had cannot be proven. My instructions had not told the auditors to stay off the live site; the checkers were.
