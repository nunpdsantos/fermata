University of Iowa classical-guitar samples (Bank B — "Iowa Raimundo 118")
==========================================================================

Source:  University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios — Musical Instrument
         Samples (MIS). Raimundo 118 classical (nylon-string) guitar,
         recorded by Lawrence Fritts, Director of the Electronic Music
         Studios, with a single Neumann KM 84 cardioid condenser microphone
         in the anechoic chamber of the Wendell Johnson Speech and Hearing
         Center at the University of Iowa.
         https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
         https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/sound%20files/MIS/Piano_Other/guitar/

License: Free for any use, without restrictions. Quoted verbatim from the MIS
         site (https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html):

           "Since 1997, these recordings have been freely available on this
            website and may be downloaded and used for any projects, without
            restrictions."

         No attribution is required; this credit is given with thanks.

Preparation (from the upstream per-string "sul" range files):
  These are the mezzo-forte (mf) dynamic takes. The upstream recordings are
  continuous chromatic ascending runs per string, one note after another with
  silence between. The 39-note bank E2-F#5 was sliced out of five of them:

    sulE (E2B2)   -> E2 F2 F#2 G2 G#2 A2 A#2 B2   (MIDI 40-47, 8 notes)
    sulA (C3B3)   -> C3 C#3                        (MIDI 48-49, first 2 only)
    sulD (D3B3)   -> D3 .. B3                       (MIDI 50-59, 10 notes)
    sulG (C4B4)   -> C4 .. B4                       (MIDI 60-71, 12 notes)
    sulB (C5Gb5)  -> C5 .. F#5                      (MIDI 72-78, 7 notes)

  Slicing: each note cut from its onset (a near-digital-silence gap separates
  notes in the source) to a 4.0 s cap, with a 0.6 s fade-out, then encoded to
  match Bank A exactly:

    ffmpeg ... -af "afade=t=out:st=3.4:d=0.6" -ar 44100 -ac 1 -b:a 128k <NOTE>.mp3

  Every slice was pitch-verified (librosa pYIN fundamental, modal semitone with
  a harmonic-series octave-error guard) to within +/-50 cents of its label
  before inclusion. Sharp note names use 's' in filenames (F#3 -> Fs3.mp3).

Note: this is the "B" bank in a temporary A/B bake-off (WS11) against the
default FreePats bank (public/samples/guitar/). The losing bank and the switch
mechanism are slated for removal once the better-sounding bank is chosen.
