Spanish Classical Guitar (nylon-string) samples
================================================

Source:  FreePats project — "Nylon-String Acoustic Guitar" (Spanish Classical
         Guitar), recorded by Roberto (zenvoid.org), version 2019-06-18.
         https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html
         https://github.com/freepats/spanish-classical-guitar

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
         No attribution is required; this credit is given with thanks.

Preparation (from the upstream FLAC files):
  39 individual notes covering the classical guitar range E2–C6
  (upstream gaps at F#2, G#2, A#2, C#3, D#3, G#4 are pitch-shifted from neighbors
  by the sampler engine). Each file:
    ffmpeg -i <NOTE>.flac -t 4.0 -af "afade=t=out:st=3.4:d=0.6" \
           -ar 44100 -ac 1 -b:a 128k <NOTE>.mp3
  Sharp note names use 's' in filenames (F#3 → Fs3.mp3).
